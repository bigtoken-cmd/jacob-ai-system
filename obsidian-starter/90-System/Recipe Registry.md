# Recipe Registry

Recipes are reusable methods, not full conversation histories.

## Promotion rule

Promote only after repeated success on independent or held-out tasks. Record the evaluator and evidence.

## Failure rule

Quarantine failed recipes with a failure code before retirement. Do not erase the reason for failure.

## Token rule

A recipe must be judged by cost per accepted result, including retries and human correction time.
