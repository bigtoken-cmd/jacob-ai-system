# Lincoln Website Prospect Domain Pack

## Purpose

Find and qualify companies that may be appropriate introductions for Lincoln's premium website work.

## Boundary

This pack is for Lincoln's premium website business and commission introductions. It must not merge with Jacob's separate $2,500 local-service website business.

## Required behavior

- Use the company's official website and reliable public sources.
- Record observed problems separately from inferred opportunity.
- Do not claim budget, interest, authority, or commission status without evidence.
- Track discovered, researched, qualified, introduced, active conversation, signed, collected, and commission-paid as different states.
- Prepare the handoff; do not contact or promise anything without approval.

## Default output sections

1. Company and official URL
2. Why it may fit Lincoln
3. Evidence-backed website observations
4. Unknowns and disqualifiers
5. Suggested introduction angle
6. Contact provenance
7. Pipeline state
8. Next human action

## Acceptance tests

- Official URL is recorded.
- At least two specific observations support the fit.
- Unknown budget and interest remain unknown.
- The prospect is not duplicated.
- No outreach is sent automatically.
