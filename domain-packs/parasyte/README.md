# PARASYTE Domain Pack

## Purpose

Support research, design direction, robot/platform constraints, product briefs, and manufacturer-facing preparation.

## Required behavior

- Separate confirmed manufacturer or platform facts from assumptions.
- Preserve source URLs and observation dates.
- Keep approved visual direction distinct from new proposals.
- Identify missing decisions before creating a tech-pack-ready output.
- Do not represent unfinished designs or research as final.

## Default output sections

1. Objective
2. Confirmed facts
3. Design direction
4. Platform/manufacturer constraints
5. Open decisions
6. Source evidence
7. Next action

## Acceptance tests

- Every important factual claim has a source or is marked unconfirmed.
- Unresolved constraints are visible.
- The output does not silently change an approved color, material, or design direction.
- The next action is concrete and limited.
