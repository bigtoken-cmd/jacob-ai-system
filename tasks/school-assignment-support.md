# Pilot Task: School assignment support

Task ID: `SCHOOL-ASSIGNMENT-SUPPORT`

## Goal

Turn one real assignment's instructions and source material into permitted study or draft support.

## Required inputs

- Exact assignment page, prompt, or export — **needed from Jacob**.
- Assignment-specific AI and resource rules — **needed from Jacob or the assignment source**.
- Rubric or source module — **needed from Jacob**.
- Desired help type, such as study explanation or permitted draft support — **needed from Jacob**.

## Required sources

- Official assignment instructions — **blocked; not supplied**.
- Assignment-specific rules about AI and permitted resources — **blocked; not supplied**.
- Rubric or required source module — **blocked; not supplied**.
- Any source material Jacob is permitted to use — **blocked; not supplied**.

## Constraints

- Verify the exact assignment and instructions before providing support.
- Follow the assignment's AI and resource restrictions; do not infer them.
- Distinguish study help, draft help, staged answers, and final submission.
- Label missing information instead of guessing.
- Do not present work as submitted or graded.
- The final submission remains Jacob's responsibility.
- Do not submit work to a school or take other external action.

## Acceptance criteria

- The exact assignment identity is verified.
- The exact assignment rules are checked and followed.
- Required prompt parts are addressed, when the prompt and rubric are available.
- No answer is presented as final when the assignment forbids AI assistance.
- Missing source information is labeled instead of guessed.
- The output clearly states what Jacob must do next.

## Human approval point

Jacob must provide and confirm the assignment rules and desired help type before work begins. Jacob must review any study or draft support and remains responsible for the final submission.

## Missing information

- **BLOCKED:** Exact assignment page, prompt, or export is unavailable.
- **BLOCKED:** Assignment-specific AI and resource rules are unavailable.
- **BLOCKED:** Rubric or source module is unavailable.
- **BLOCKED:** Desired help type is unspecified.
