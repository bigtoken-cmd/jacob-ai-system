# Pilot Task: PARASYTE research-to-brief

Task ID: `PARASYTE-RESEARCH-TO-BRIEF`

## Goal

Turn a selected raw research packet into a source-backed PARASYTE product brief.

## Required inputs

- Selected PARASYTE research question or scope — **needed from Jacob**.
- Actual robot or platform — **needed from Jacob**.
- Approved design direction — **needed from Jacob**.
- Manufacturer requirements and constraints — **needed from Jacob**.

## Required sources

- Original PARASYTE research packet, including source URLs and observation dates — **blocked; not supplied**.
- Relevant robot/platform documentation — **blocked; platform not selected**.
- Approved PARASYTE design notes or decisions — **blocked; not supplied**.
- Manufacturer requirements — **blocked; not supplied**.

## Constraints

- Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
- Preserve source URLs and observation dates.
- Keep approved visual direction distinct from new proposals.
- Identify missing decisions before creating a tech-pack-ready output.
- Do not label unfinished research or concepts as final.
- Do not modify the live Obsidian vault or take external action.

## Acceptance criteria

- Confirmed facts and assumptions are separated.
- Every important factual claim has a source and date or is marked unconfirmed.
- Platform, manufacturer, and other constraints are visible.
- Open decisions and unresolved requirements are visible.
- No unfinished concept is labeled final.
- The next action is concrete and limited.

## Human approval point

Jacob must provide or confirm the scope, platform, approved design direction, and manufacturer requirements before work begins. Jacob must review and approve the brief before any manufacturer-facing use or external action.

## Missing information

- **BLOCKED:** No selected research scope or raw research packet.
- **BLOCKED:** No actual robot/platform identified.
- **BLOCKED:** No approved design direction supplied.
- **BLOCKED:** No manufacturer requirements supplied.
